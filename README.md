# Financial Risk & Sales Performance Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze financial performance, outstanding balances, portfolio risk, and default rates across multiple branches, sales platforms, and business channels.

The project combines business performance and credit risk analysis to support data-driven decision-making and identify areas requiring management attention.

## Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Visualization
- KPI Development
- Financial Performance Analysis
- Portfolio Risk Analysis
- Sales Performance Analysis
- Interactive Dashboard Design

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | 249.95M EGP |
| Total Cash Inflow | 110.48M EGP |
| Average Order Value | 49.99K EGP |
| Total Outstanding Balance | 69.73M EGP |
| Portfolio at Risk (PAR >30 Days) | 14.77% |
| Default Rate (>90 Days) | 4.84% |

## Dashboard Overview

### 1. Business Overview

Provides an overview of financial and commercial performance through:

- Revenue distribution across sales platforms
- Revenue and cash inflow comparisons by branch
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Outstanding balances
- Portfolio at Risk and default rates by branch
- Interactive filters for year, branch, and sales platform

### 2. Risk Details

Focuses on credit risk exposure and overdue balances through:

- PAR >30 Days and default rates by sales platform
- Risk comparisons between retail and wholesale channels
- Number of orders by aging bucket
- Outstanding balances by aging bucket
- Interactive risk analysis by branch and business channel

## Key Business Insights

- **Revenue Performance:** Total revenue reached 249.95M EGP, with the Web platform contributing approximately 36.38% of revenue.
- **Outstanding Exposure:** Total outstanding balances reached 69.73M EGP.
- **Portfolio Risk:** The overall PAR >30 Days rate was 14.77%, while the default rate was 4.84%.
- **Branch Risk:** BR-5 recorded the highest PAR >30 Days rate at 18.73% and the highest default rate at 6.48%.
- **Sales Platform Risk:** Store recorded the highest PAR >30 Days rate at 15.62%, while Web had the highest default rate at 5.23%.
- **Business Channel Risk:** Retail recorded a higher PAR >30 Days rate (15.36%) than Wholesale (12.43%).
- **Overdue Balances:** The 90+ days aging bucket accounted for approximately 4M EGP in outstanding balances.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings:

- Prioritize risk monitoring for branches with elevated PAR and default rates, particularly BR-5.
- Review credit and collection strategies for high-risk sales platforms and business channels.
- Strengthen follow-up procedures for overdue balances.
- Monitor outstanding balances by aging bucket to identify deterioration in repayment performance.
- Combine revenue and risk KPIs when evaluating branch performance.
